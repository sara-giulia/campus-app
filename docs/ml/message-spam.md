# Message spam model — model card

The message spam model gives the probability that a chat message between a buyer and
a seller is spam or a scam, from its text alone. `StartConversation` and `SendMessage`
ask it for every message (see "Spam in messages" in the README). The saved model lives
at `ml/models/message-spam.zip`.

## Why a model of its own

The spam model of the listings (`spam-detection.zip`) was tried first on chat messages
and does not fit them: it learned from a title, a description and a price, and short
chat lines are none of these. Its rules also mark a phone number or "WhatsApp" as spam,
which is normal between two students who meet on campus. On the same messages as below
(listing model at its threshold 0.35, text as the description, with its rules):

| | Precision | Recall | F1 | Normal messages flagged |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Test part | 0.199 | 0.701 | 0.310 | 274 of 637 |
| Hand-written | 0.633 | 0.760 | 0.691 | 11 of 45 |

## What the model does

- **Task:** binary classification (`IsSpam`) of one message.
- **Features:** `Text` through `FeaturizeText` (words and pairs of words, groups of
  three letters, lower case, no diacritics).
- **Trainer:** SDCA logistic regression, seed 2026, `NumberOfThreads = 1`; the same
  pipeline as the spam model of the listings, without the price.
- **Output:** a probability between 0 and 1, read with two thresholds:
  - **0.60, warning** (`Ml:MessageSpam:Threshold`): the message is sent but shown with a
    warning;
  - **0.95, block** (`Ml:MessageSpam:BlockThreshold`): the message is not sent (400
    `Message.Spam`).
- In the API, `MessageSpamRules.cs` also marks a message as suspicious (never blocked) for
  a link, card data or a CVV, a code from an SMS or the bank, money asked in advance,
  and the usual bait (easy money, crypto, loans, gift cards). A phone number, WhatsApp or
  an e-mail address is not a rule here.

## Data

- `ml/data/messages.csv`, generated by `MessageGenerator.cs` from `MessageTemplates.cs`
  with its own `Random(2028)` (so `listings.csv` does not change): **5000 messages, 750
  spam (15%)**, **76 templates**: 47 kinds of normal messages (availability, price, offer
  and counter-offer, place and time, condition, photos, reservation, payment at the
  meeting, phone / WhatsApp / e-mail, courier with payment on delivery, thanks, sold,
  cancel…) and 29 spam patterns (money in advance for the courier, a fake courier or
  payment link, card data, an SMS code, overpayment, "a relative brings it", easy money,
  work from home, crypto, trading, giveaway, loans, buying accounts, channels, betting,
  pyramid, fake CampusMarket support, exam answers, surveys, English job, gift cards,
  spelled and short links, referral codes, fake escrow, too good to be true, fake bank
  call, parcel insurance, "I buy everything").
- **Hard normal messages on purpose:** normal templates use the words of the scams (card,
  code, link, advance, courier, transfer: "Pot plăti cu cardul când ne vedem?", "Codul de
  la interfon e 23", "Nu cer avans", "Am făcut transferul, mersi"), so the model learns
  what is asked, not one word.
- Each row: a phrasing of the template, its slots filled (item, price, place, time,
  phone, e-mail, link, handle, amount), then the noise of real chat: no diacritics (55%),
  lower case (30%), short forms (30%), a typo (15%), an emoji (10%), a greeting or a
  closing line; 10% of the spam is loud (CAPITALS and "!!!").
- `ml/data/messages-external.csv`: **70 messages written by hand** (45 normal, 25 spam),
  not made from the templates, only for evaluation.

## Split and thresholds

- **By template.** The normal templates and the spam templates are each sorted, shuffled
  with `Random(2026)`, the first 15% go to **test** (11 templates, 734 messages, 97 spam)
  and the rest (65 templates, 4266 messages) are dealt into **5 folds**. A template is in
  one place only, so test and every fold hold kinds of messages and spam patterns the
  model never saw.
- **Thresholds by cross-validation.** Each fold is scored by a model trained on the other
  four; the thresholds are chosen on those predictions (4266 messages), not on the test:
  - warning: the best **F0.5**, which counts precision twice as much as recall. A warning
    on a normal message hurts the chat between two students, and the clear scams are also
    caught by the rules;
  - block: the smallest threshold, not under the warning one, at which **no normal
    message** of the folds is blocked.
- The saved model is then trained on all 65 templates and measured **once** on test.

Cross-validation (each fold scored by a model that did not see its templates):

| Threshold | Precision | Recall | F1 | Normal flagged | Spam missed |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.20 | 0.628 | 0.861 | 0.726 | 333 | 91 |
| 0.40 | 0.684 | 0.723 | 0.703 | 218 | 181 |
| **0.60 (warning)** | 0.763 | 0.606 | 0.676 | 123 | 257 |
| 0.80 | 0.828 | 0.398 | 0.538 | 54 | 393 |
| 0.90 | 0.985 | 0.309 | 0.471 | 3 | 451 |
| **0.95 (block)** | 1.000 | 0.234 | 0.380 | 0 | 500 |

The folds are harder than real use: each one leaves out whole kinds of messages, so a
normal message of a kind never seen ("send it by courier, I pay on delivery") looks new
to the model.

## Metrics

| | Precision | Recall | F1 | Normal flagged | Spam missed |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Test, model at 0.60 | 0.975 | 0.814 | 0.888 | 2 of 637 | 18 of 97 |
| Test, rules only | 1.000 | 0.495 | 0.662 | 0 | 49 |
| **Test, model or rules (the API)** | **0.978** | **0.938** | **0.958** | 2 | 6 |
| Test, blocked at 0.95 | 1.000 | 0.216 | — | **0** | — |
| Hand-written, model at 0.60 | 1.000 | 0.880 | 0.936 | 0 of 45 | 3 of 25 |
| Hand-written, rules only | 1.000 | 0.600 | 0.750 | 0 | 10 |
| **Hand-written, model or rules (the API)** | **1.000** | **1.000** | **1.000** | 0 | 0 |
| Hand-written, blocked at 0.95 | 1.000 | 0.480 | — | **0** | — |

Test at the model's own 0.5: accuracy 0.967, AUC 0.994, PR-AUC 0.971, ECE 0.068.

**Be careful with the hand-written 1.000:** the rules were written after the model was
measured on that set, and three of its rules (an SMS code, "enter your card", money in
advance) cover exactly the three messages the model missed there. The test part was not
used to write the rules; its 0.958 is the more honest number for the model and the rules
together.

## What it gets wrong

- **On test,** the 2 normal messages flagged are "send it by courier, pay on delivery"
  messages (`msg-shipping-legit`): the closest normal kind to the scam "pay the courier
  in advance". The 18 spam missed at 0.60 are fake CampusMarket support (12 of 18 rows of
  `spam-fake-support`) and referral codes (6 of 29 rows of `spam-referral-app`), patterns
  with words the model did not see; the rules catch the ones with a link.
- **Hand-written,** the model alone missed: "Trimite-mi 150 lei avans pe Revolut ca să
  ți-l păstrez" (0.43), "Ți-a venit un SMS cu un cod? Dictează-mi-l" (0.19), and an
  English scam with a link and card details (0.04, almost no English in the data).
- **Blocking is rare on purpose:** at 0.95 only about a quarter to a half of the spam is
  blocked; the rest is sent with a warning. Blocking a normal message would be worse
  than letting a scam through with a warning.

## Limits

- The data is generated from templates; real chat is more varied. The hand-written set
  is small (70 messages).
- Only Romanian (and a little English); other languages are not covered.
- A scam spread over several messages (first friendly, then the link) is seen one
  message at a time.
- Nobody reads the conversations (an Admin cannot either), so there is no label from
  real use yet; a "this is not spam" button for the receiver would give the feedback
  loop real examples.
